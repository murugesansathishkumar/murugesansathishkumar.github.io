Sathishkumar Murugesan

Research Assistant Professor
Department of Mathematics
SRM Institute of Science and Technology

My research is centered on the analysis and design of intelligent control strategies for dynamical systems operating under uncertainty, limited communication, and adversarial conditions. I develop stability- and optimization-based methods for resilient networked control, autonomous systems, multi-agent systems, and learning-based control, with particular emphasis on ensuring reliability, security, and performance in interconnected cyber-physical systems.

Research Interests

I am interested in mathematical and computational methods for the analysis and control of complex dynamical systems under uncertainty, communication constraints, faults, and cyber-attacks. I work across:

  • Robust & Resilient Control
  • Networked & Multi-Agent Systems
  • Event-Triggered Mechanisms
  • Stochastic Systems
  • Stability Theory
  • Reinforcement Learning

Applications include:

  • Autonomous Vehicles
  • Unmanned Aerial & Surface Vehicles
  • Robotic Systems
  • Cyber-Physical Systems
